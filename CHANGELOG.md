# Changelog

All notable changes to MRRadar are recorded here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.1.1] — 2026-11-07

### Added
- Check for updates and install them from the app, with checksum, signature and notarization checks.

### Changed
- Settings now look like Remote Code's, with a sidebar and panes.
- The selected app stays clearly marked in the sidebar.

## [0.1.0] — 2026-11-07

### Added
- Menu bar panel and a window with every app side by side, and one app in detail.
- MRR forecast, month-end pace, quick ratio, implied LTV, trial pipeline, unusual days,
  best weekdays, a retention heatmap, and revenue by country and store.
- Alerts with Mac notifications and your own thresholds.
- Setup that installs the RevenueCat CLI, signs in and picks your apps before the app opens.
- A local hub for Remote Code.
- Ten languages.
