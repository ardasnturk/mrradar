## MRRadar 0.1.0

The first release. MRRadar puts all your RevenueCat apps on one radar, in your menu bar.

* Compare every app: MRR, change, quick ratio, churn, implied LTV and month-end pace.
* See an MRR forecast, unusual days, best weekdays, retention, countries and stores.
* Get alerts for new subscribers, falling MRR, milestones, refunds and churn.

MRRadar is free, open source and read-only. It needs macOS 15 and a RevenueCat account, and sets up the rest itself. It isn't affiliated with RevenueCat.
